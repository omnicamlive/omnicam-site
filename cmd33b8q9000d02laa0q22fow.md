## **Body:**

### 🚀 What is OmniCam?

**OmniCam** is a powerful Android application designed for **background video recording** using **both front and back cameras simultaneously**, even when the screen is off or the device is locked. It also enables **live RTMP streaming**, and offers options to embed **timestamp** and **location overlays** directly on the video feed.

This makes it an ideal tool for journalists, security professionals, legal observers, researchers, and anyone who needs uninterrupted, discreet video recording.

---

### 🎯 Why OmniCam?

Traditional camera apps limit users to one camera at a time and often interrupt recording when the app is closed or the screen is turned off. OmniCam changes that.

* ✅ **Dual-camera support** (front and rear simultaneously on supported devices)
    
* ✅ **Runs in the background** with the screen off and phone locked
    
* ✅ **Live stream to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram via RTMP URLs**
    
* ✅ **Add timestamp & location watermarks** for authenticity
    
* ✅ **Volume key recording control** via Accessibility Service
    
* ✅ **Schedule recordings** in advance
    
* ✅ **Stores video locally** while streaming
    

---

### 🔐 Privacy & Transparency First

We understand the sensitivity of background recording and streaming features. That’s why OmniCam:

* Always shows a **persistent notification** while recording
    
* Does **not record without user action**
    
* Complies with **Google Play policies** including **Foreground Service Permissions**
